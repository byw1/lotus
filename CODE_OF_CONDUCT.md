# Code of conduct

The Lotus Festival exists to bring people together across cultures. This project
holds to the same standard.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming, patient and generous in interpretation. Many contributors here
  are volunteers, first-time contributors, or festival staff who are not
  developers.
- Assume good faith, and give feedback on the work rather than the person.
- Be careful and specific when discussing culture, heritage, language or
  religion. Ask questions instead of assuming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, slurs, demeaning comments, or discrimination on the basis of race,
ethnicity, national origin, immigration status, religion, disability, age,
gender identity or expression, or sexual orientation. Publishing others' private
information. Sustained disruption.

Racist or "orientalist" caricature of the cultures this festival honors is
harassment, and will be treated as such.

## Reporting

Email `lotus.festival@lacity.org` with `CONDUCT` in the subject line. Reports are
handled privately. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban a contributor.

This code applies in this repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any
project space.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
